⚖️ Election Laws

The Representation of the People Act, 1951 governs the conduct of elections. Section 8 details disqualifications on conviction for certain offenses to keep criminals out of politics.

Candidates must strictly adhere to spending limits (e.g., ₹95 Lakh for major states in Lok Sabha). Daily accounts must be maintained and submitted to the ECI.

The MCC ensures a level playing field. It bans using religion/caste for votes, distributing money/liquor, and misusing government machinery.

Paying media to disguise advertisements as news is an electoral offense. All political ads require pre-certification from the MCMC.
